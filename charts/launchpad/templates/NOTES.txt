launchpad deployed successfully.

Usage:
  Forward the service to your local machine:
    kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "launchpad.fullname" . }} 8081:{{ .Values.service.port }}

  Then in another terminal, smoke-test the endpoints:
    curl http://localhost:8081/health        # expect 200 when launchpad's MongoDB is reachable (503 until Phase 9)
    curl http://localhost:8081/diagnostics    # expect the diagnostics dashboard page
    curl http://localhost:8081/api/status     # expect platform status JSON

  Press Ctrl-C in the port-forward terminal to stop.

  To remove all chart resources:
    helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }}
